# Picuki alternative: PicMany

**Looking for Picuki?** Picuki's Instagram viewer is no longer at its address. When PicMany checked on 29 October 2026,
`picuki.com` and `www.picuki.com` both redirected to `tikvib.com`, a TikTok tool.

**[PicMany](https://picmany.com) is a free Picuki alternative** for public Instagram profiles, posts, reels and
stories, with no Instagram account and no login.

## What you used Picuki for, on PicMany

| Picuki feature | On PicMany |
|---|---|
| View a profile | ✅ Search a username, or go to `picmany.com/username`: name, bio, links, counts, highlights and recent posts |
| View a post | ✅ `picmany.com/p/CODE`, with the same code as the Instagram link |
| Reels | ✅ [Popular reels](https://picmany.com/reels), and every account's own reels in its **Reels** tab |
| Stories | ✅ Best effort: a public account's stories load in the **Stories** tab of its profile |
| Hashtags and topics | ✅ As Instagram's popular results: `picmany.com/popular/topic` shows the posts Instagram ranks as popular for a term (not every post with the tag) |
| Downloads | ❌ Not offered; the photos and videos belong to the people who posted them |
| Photo editor | ❌ Not offered |

**Also on PicMany:** [lists of the most followed Instagram accounts](https://picmany.com/most-followed) by topic or
country, and follower history charts for public accounts with at least 1,000 followers.

## How to switch

- Search a **username** or **topic** on [picmany.com](https://picmany.com).
- Or paste an **Instagram link** into the search box.
- Or swap the host: `instagram.com/natgeo` → `picmany.com/natgeo`.

## Questions

**Why is Picuki not working?** Its viewer is no longer at picuki.com; the address forwards to a TikTok tool. PicMany has
no connection with Picuki and cannot say whether it will come back.

**Is PicMany free?** Yes. No sign-up, no subscription, no app.

**Do I need an Instagram account?** No. You never log in on PicMany, and it never asks for your Instagram password.

**Will the account know I looked?** No. You are not logged in, so there is no account of yours for the visit to show up
under.

**Can I see private accounts?** No. PicMany shows public content only, like Picuki did.

PicMany is not affiliated with Picuki, Instagram or Meta. Picuki is named here only to help people who are looking for
it. The same page on the site: [picmany.com/picuki-alternative](https://picmany.com/picuki-alternative).
