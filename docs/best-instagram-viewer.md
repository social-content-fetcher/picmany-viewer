# What makes the best Instagram viewer

There are many sites that call themselves an "Instagram viewer". Use this checklist to tell a good one from a bad one,
and see how **[PicMany](https://picmany.com)** measures up.

## The checklist

| What to check | Why it matters | PicMany |
|---|---|---|
| **No login, no password** | A viewer that asks for your Instagram password can take over your account | ✅ Never asks for any login |
| **No app or extension to install** | Extensions and apps can read far more than a web page can | ✅ Works in any browser |
| **Public content only, said plainly** | No website can show a private account's posts; a site that promises it is a scam | ✅ Public only, stated on every relevant page |
| **No surveys or "human verification" walls** | These are the classic signs of a scam site | ✅ None |

| **Fresh data** | Old copies mislead | ✅ Pages are at most about an hour old; deleted posts disappear |

| **A privacy policy and a removal process** | Shows the site takes account owners seriously | ✅ [Privacy Policy](https://picmany.com/privacy) and [Remove a profile](https://picmany.com/remove-profile) |

| **Free** | You should not pay to look at public posts | ✅ Free, no subscription |

## Red flags

- **"View private Instagram accounts"**: not possible without the owner's approval. Sites that claim it either show
  nothing, show someone else's public posts, or try to get your data.
- **"Enter your Instagram username and password"**: never do this on a third-party site.
- **"Complete one offer to unlock"**: survey walls make money for the site and give you nothing.
- **Mandatory downloads**: a viewer does not need you to install anything.
- **No privacy policy, no contact address**: you cannot know what happens to your visit.
