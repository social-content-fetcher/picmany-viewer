# What is PicMany?

**[PicMany](https://picmany.com) is a free, read-only Instagram viewer.** It shows public Instagram profiles, posts,
reels, stories and highlights in any web browser, without an Instagram account, a login or an app.

PicMany is an independent website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

## What you can see on PicMany

| Page | Address | What is on it |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | `picmany.com/username` | Name, bio and links, follower, following and post counts, recent posts, highlights, related accounts |
| Post | `picmany.com/p/CODE` | Photo, video or carousel, caption, date, like and comment counts where Instagram shows them, comment preview |
| Reel | `picmany.com/reel/CODE` | The reel and its details |
| Stories | `picmany.com/username` → **Stories** tab | The account's current stories, when Instagram makes them available |
| Popular topic | `picmany.com/popular/topic` | The posts Instagram ranks as popular for a search term, with related searches |
| Popular reels | [`picmany.com/reels`](https://picmany.com/reels) | Reels that are popular on Instagram right now |
| Rankings | [`picmany.com/most-followed`](https://picmany.com/most-followed) | Most followed accounts by topic or country |

Public accounts with at least 1,000 followers also get a **follower history** chart, so you can see how their
follower, following and post counts change over time. [How we count](https://picmany.com/how-we-count) explains where
every number comes from.

## How PicMany works

When you open a page, PicMany's server fetches the public information for it from Instagram, the same information
Instagram shows a visitor who is not logged in, and builds the page from it. The result is kept for a short time,
usually up to an hour, so the next visitor gets it at once and Instagram is asked as rarely as possible.

- Pages are **rendered on the server**, so they open fast on any phone or computer and work without installing
  anything.
- **Photos** come through PicMany's own address. **Videos and stories** play straight from Instagram's servers.
- Stories and highlights are fetched separately when you open them, so they can take a few seconds to appear.
- PicMany is **not an archive**. When a post or an account is deleted on Instagram, it disappears from PicMany too,
  normally within about an hour.

## What PicMany is not

- **Not Instagram.** You cannot post, like, comment, follow or send messages on PicMany.
- **Not a private account viewer.** No website can show the posts of a private Instagram account, and any site that
  claims to is not telling the truth. On PicMany a private account shows only the name, picture, bio and counts that
  Instagram itself shows publicly.
- **Not a downloader.** PicMany has no download buttons. The photos and videos belong to the people who posted them.
- **Not a data API.** PicMany is a website for people; see
  [Why use PicMany directly](why-use-picmany-directly.md).

## Who uses PicMany

- People who **don't have an Instagram account**, or don't want to log in, and still want to see a public profile.
- People who want to **look at a profile or a story without being seen** in a viewer list.
- People who got an Instagram link and **don't want to install the app** to open it.
- Anyone who wants to check a creator's, brand's or athlete's **follower count and how it changes**.
- People who used [Picuki](picuki-alternative.md) and are looking for an alternative.

## Privacy for visitors and account owners

You never log in, so there is no Instagram account on your side. PicMany runs no advertising trackers; it counts visits
with Google Analytics, and the [Privacy Policy](https://picmany.com/privacy) lists exactly what is processed.

PicMany shows only what a public Instagram profile already shows to everyone. If you own an account and would rather
it did not appear on PicMany, [Remove a profile](https://picmany.com/remove-profile) explains how to have it taken
down.

## Start

Open **[picmany.com](https://picmany.com)**, type a username or a topic, or paste an Instagram link.
