# Anonymous Instagram story viewer

**[PicMany](https://picmany.com) lets you watch the stories and highlights of public Instagram accounts without
logging in**, so your account never shows up in the story's viewer list.

## How to view an Instagram story anonymously

1. Open **[picmany.com](https://picmany.com)**.
2. Search the **username** of a public account, or open `picmany.com/username` directly.
3. Open the **Stories** tab on the profile. Current stories load in a few seconds.
4. **Highlights** sit on the profile too: open one to watch its stories.

Got a story link like `instagram.com/stories/username/1234567890/`? Paste it into PicMany's search box, or run
`picmany-viewer open <link>`: it opens the account's profile, where the Stories tab is.

## Why your name does not appear in the viewers list

Instagram lists the **signed-in accounts** that watched a story. On PicMany you are never signed in to Instagram, and
PicMany never asks for your Instagram password, so there is no account of yours to list.

## Good to know

- **Public accounts only.** Stories of private accounts are not shown, on PicMany or on any honest viewer.
- **Best effort.** Stories are fetched when you open the tab, and Instagram does not always make them available. If
  they do not load, try again a little later.
- **24 hours.** Instagram stories disappear after 24 hours unless the owner adds them to a highlight. PicMany is not an
  archive and does not keep expired stories.
- **Videos play from Instagram's servers**, like any embedded video.
- **No downloads.** Stories are for watching; they belong to the people who posted them.

## Stories, without the app

PicMany works in any browser on iPhone, Android, Windows, macOS and Linux. There is nothing to install and no browser
extension to add.
